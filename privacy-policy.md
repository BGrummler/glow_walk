Privacy Policy

This app is designed to display a red blinking light on the smartphone screen so that the user can be more visible in the dark.

The app does not collect, store, or process any personal data.

No user data is transmitted to the developer or to any third parties. The app does not use analytics tools, tracking technologies, or advertising services.

The app only uses basic device functionality required to display the visual blinking signal on the screen.

No personal information is requested or stored by the app.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the app’s Google Play Store page.
